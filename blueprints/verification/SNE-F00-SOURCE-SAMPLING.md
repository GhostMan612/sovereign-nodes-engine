# F-00 — Reproducible review sampling design

## Mandatory coverage, not random alone

- **100% of P0:** claims F00-C01 through F00-C11. Include path existence and exactly cited line sections, negative cross-read of producer/consumer, and complete surrounding parser method for `ScanInt`/`ScanRaw`.
- **Targeted P1:** F00-C12 through F00-C16, with at least GZDoom, UZDoom and BiasedDoom represented; scrutinize current vs historical option defaults and policy wording.
- **Historical audit sample:** trace each of WO-A through WO-F to its publication commit and acceptance record. Independently verify one high-impact finding and one checksum or cross-reference from each selected work order. If a historical ledger needs correction, issue a new superseding record; don't rewrite the old one.
- **Uncertainty sample:** select one assertion classified inference or `DOC-CLAIM`, and explain which concrete run would falsify it.

## Fair sampling rules

1. Freeze sampling procedure before inspecting outcomes and retain IDs of checked lines and failed lookups.
2. Include negative assertions (missing files and absence of a behavior) only after checking exact path, case-sensitive tree membership and referenced wrappers where possible.
3. Never inflate sample coverage to “full repository audit” if only selected files were checked.
4. Pair a source claim with a potential counterclaim and its primary location, not just an approving quotation.
5. Audit substitutions: did someone quietly compare a patched fixture or a new engine revision while calling it the original?

## Sampling report output

Claim IDs reviewed, successful and failed fetch counts, blob mismatch count, false-positive controls, excluded sources, reviewer signature and unresolved questions; do not derive engine performance or legal clearance from this review.
