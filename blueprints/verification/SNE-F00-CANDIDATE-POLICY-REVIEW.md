# F-00 — Upstream contribution policy vs downstream rights

## UZDoom pinned policy review

`https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom/blob/dbedcc894fb6866c48520cf27ae773f9957024b9/CONTRIBUTING.md#L89-L138` (Git blob `84621215c99b1da9af913d082e65e20b6d725175`) disallows AI-generated and AI-assisted **upstream code submissions**, including comments/code created with AI help. Its art section also disallows submitted generated art. This is a maintainer **contribution rule**, not evidence that independent GPL-compliant downstream forks are categorically prohibited.

## Distinct legal questions for the reviewer

- What versioned upstream license texts and per-file copyright notices bind any proposed fork?
- What is the separate upstream PR policy and does any code plan involve sending contributions upstream? **Do not submit AI-authored patches to UZDoom upstream.**
- What names/logos/brand assets and included resources could be restricted despite source-code GPL terms?
- What third-party libraries, asset packages and transitive dependencies require their own notices or redistribution checks?
- Is the actual sustainable offline/local development workflow feasible without upstream contributions? This is a maintenance and operator choice, not a claim of legal clearance.

## Decision

`G2 NOT CLEARED`, `G7 INDEPENDENT CHECK PENDING`. Record rights conclusions only after a qualified independent license/asset review tied to exact distribution contents. No commercial IWAD is included or authorized by this documentation.
