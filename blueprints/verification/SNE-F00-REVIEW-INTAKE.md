## Primary review sequence

1. Compare F-00 source pins and blob hashes with actual immutable Git file content independently.
2. Audit P0 source claims and find at least one counterargument or missing condition for each critical conclusion.
3. Review candidate-source CMake/policy rows without treating them as successful configure/build or legal approval.
4. Record any bad path, wrong line, misleading paraphrase, missing parser behavior, or circular reference as a new issue.
5. Review unresolved risks with the operator; issue an evidence-backed reviewer decision in the blank review template.
6. Preserve technical G3–G6 as NOT RUN, even when all source claims are supported. Do not sign a foundation selection.

## Required evidence hygiene

No original IWAD bytes, credentials, real genealogical identities, raw AI conversation transcripts, local drive usernames or personal reviewer contact in published Markdown/JSON. An authorized reviewer may keep private signed source notes elsewhere under operator control.
