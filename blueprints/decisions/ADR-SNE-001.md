# ADR-SNE-001 — Hybrid compatibility-first direction

- Status: PROPOSED
- Decision: pursue a modular Doom-compatible derivative **subject to** foundation audit and operator approval.
- Alternatives: from-scratch engine; Godot-based gameplay; unmodified GZDoom; aggressive upstream fork.
- Consequences: lower initial compatibility risk, ongoing upstream maintenance, GPL/license review required.
- Revisit when: evidence disproves feasibility on target hardware or Tier-A compatibility.
